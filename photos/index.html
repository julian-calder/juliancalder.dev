Stavanger, Norway

Jul 18 - 21, 2026

After a few days in Tromsø, we flew the length of the country to reach the second stop on our trip – Stavanger. Despite being Norway's oil capital and third largest city, Stavanger was able to feel both cozy and metropolitan, with quiet, cobblestone streets weaving throughout the charming city center. One defining feature of Stavanger was the overwhelming number of predatory seagulls and the general aroma of saltwater that wafted in the air, which gave the city a distinctly coastal feeling. Many of these photos depict Preikestolen, or "Pulpit's Rock", as pictured from atop the rock and from the fjord below.

Tromsø, Norway

Jul 16-18, 2026

The first photographed destination on my trip to Scandinavia in the summer of 2026 was Tromsø (after a brief stop in Stockholm to relive the glory days of my semester studying abroad there). It was so disorienting and fascinating to experience the midnight sun, as my only other visit above the Arctic Circle happened in early December, where there was nearly 24 hours of darkness. The far northern latitude of Tromsø very much defined the overall feel of the town and our time in it, and provided an experience unlike anything I had seen before.

Great Sand Dunes National Park

Aug 4 – 5, 2023

I drove down to Great Sand Dunes National Park on Friday, and because it was the anniversary of the passing of the Great American Outdoors Act entrance to the park was free! We arrived just before sunset and had the spectacular experience of hiking up to the top of the highest dune as the sun was going down. The lack of moonlight made for the most amazing night of stargazing that I've ever had. Absolutely worth the visit.
